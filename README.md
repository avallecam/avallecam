# Hi there 👋

## 🇵🇪 **Andree Valle-Campos**

Hi! I’m Andree, an epidemiologist from Peru working in outbreak analytics, serology, and health data science. I bridge epidemiology and quantitative methods to make outbreak response more accessible through teaching and open science.

Most recently, at the **London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (LSHTM)**, I developed and organised the short course on outbreak analytics and applied modelling in R, building on the tutorials and how-to guides I wrote for the [Epiverse-TRACE](https://epiverse-trace.github.io) project. I’ve trained over 300 epidemiologists across five continents.

I also develop and contribute to R packages that help field epidemiologists analyse line lists, social contact surveys, and serological data to produce real-time estimates and scenarios. I’m currently open to consultancies, roles with national health agencies, and curriculum development in infectious disease dynamics, based in Peru or remotely. If you’re working in this space, I’d love to connect.

My website contains **links** to all my (reusable) scientific
contributions. Take a look: [https://avallecam.github.io/](https://avallecam.github.io/)
